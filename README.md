# Dealt — A to-do list you play

Dealt turns your to-do list into a card game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Every task is a card in your hand: play it onto the table when it's done, earn XP and level up.

### How do I complete a task?

Tap a card to select it, then press Play. You can also drag a card up onto the table.

### How do I edit or delete a card?

Long-press a card (double-click on Mac) to open it. To bin a card without completing it, select it and hold the Discard button.

### How do repeating cards work?

Set Repeat when editing a card. Once you play it, the card leaves your hand and comes back by itself on the next day it's due.

### My tasks aren't syncing between devices

Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and that iCloud Drive is enabled. Changes usually sync within a minute or two.

### Can I replay the tutorial?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear button) and press Replay next to Tutorial.

### What does Dealt Pro include?

Dealt is free to play, including iCloud sync. Dealt Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks:

- Up to 10 categories (the free version has 3)
- Subcategories
- Any board colour from the full spectrum, plus your own table mix
- Your choice of XP bar colour

One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac, with no subscription.

### I bought Dealt Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open Settings (the gear button) and press Restore next to Dealt Pro, using the same Apple Account you bought it with. This also unlocks Pro on a new device.

### Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Privacy policy

*Last updated: 12 October 2026*

Dealt is a to-do list app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: **Dealt does not collect any data about you.**

### Who is responsible

Dealt is made by Alexander Keeling, an independent developer in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for this policy. Contact: [alexk3321@gmail.com](mailto:alexk3321@gmail.com).

### What Dealt stores

The tasks, categories, settings and progress you create in Dealt are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, Dealt also keeps a copy in your own iCloud account so your tasks stay in sync across your devices. That copy is held by Apple under your Apple Account and is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer of Dealt cannot see or access it.

### What Dealt doesn't do

- No accounts or sign-up
- No analytics, tracking or advertising
- No third-party services or SDKs
- No personal information is sent to the developer or anyone else

### Purchases

Dealt Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether Dealt Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account. The developer never sees your payment details, name or Apple Account. Purchases are covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### If you contact support

If you email for help, your email address and message are used only to reply to you. They are never shared or used for marketing, and are deleted once your question is resolved, unless you ask for them to be kept. The lawful basis for this is legitimate interests: answering the question you sent.

### Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in the app from Settings → Reset tasks and xp. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To remove the iCloud copy, use Reset tasks and xp while signed in to iCloud, or manage your iCloud storage in your device's Settings.

### Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law, you have the right to ask what personal information is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because Dealt collects nothing, the only information that could be held is a support email you've sent. To make a request, email the address above.

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk), or to the data protection authority in your country.

### Children

Dealt collects no data from anyone, including children.

### Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
